# GardenEssentials Wiki

GardenEssentials provides the everyday player commands for The Garden SMP and the Minecraft side of the Iris account-link and XP bridge.

## Main systems

- Balance and payments
- Spawn and warps
- TPA
- Private messages and reply
- Back and AFK
- Staff utilities
- Bug and player reports to Iris
- Minecraft to Discord account linking
- Minecraft playtime XP for linked Iris accounts
